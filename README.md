# Renewed Vision Church Community Website

A modern, welcoming, and inspiring web application for **Renewed Vision Church Community** — a Christ-centered global church community gathering inside **Roblox** and **Discord Server**.

## About Our Church & Leadership

- **Founders:** Gideon and Kevin
- **Co-Founder:** Adam
- **Mission:** Preaching the Gospel of Jesus Christ, transforming lives, and helping others through benevolence and financial support.
- **Global Organization:** Active charitable fund providing donations and support to **small churches worldwide** and **orphanages**.
- **Gathering Place:** **Roblox Church Sanctuary Experience** & **Discord Server**.
- **Weekly Service:** **Sunday Prayer Service at 3:00 PM IST** (Roblox 3D Sanctuary & Discord Stage).

## Features & Structure

- **Announcement Bar**: Sunday Prayer Service schedule (Sundays at 3:00 PM IST), Roblox & Discord alerts, and global missions updates.
- **Fixed & Sticky Navbar**: Clean single-tier header with logo, smooth-scrolling section links, Sunday Prayer service badge, and direct **Discord Server** & **Roblox Church** action buttons.
- **Hero Carousel**: High-impact modern sanctuary and community imagery with auto-slide transitions, welcoming vision statements, and sub-hero Roblox/Discord tiles.
- **Who We Are Section**: Dedicated leadership and mission section answering:
  1. *Who are we?* — Founders Gideon & Kevin, and Co-Founder Adam.
  2. *Why we created this church?* — Preaching the Gospel, transforming lives, helping others with financial support.
  3. *Do we have an organization?* — Yes, raising and directing funds to small churches worldwide and orphanages.
- **Core Values**: "Rooted in Christ. Bound in Love. Sent to Serve."
- **Ministries**: Worship & Creative Arts, Life Groups & Fellowship, NextGen Youth & Kids, Compassion & Outreach.
- **How to Join in Roblox & Discord**: 4-step onboarding guide on joining the Discord Server, launching the Roblox 3D Sanctuary, attending the Sunday Prayer Service at 3:00 PM IST, and participating in community game nights and voice hangouts.
- **Inspirational Invitation**: Full-width parallax banner with a glassmorphism card welcoming seekers and believers from across the globe.
- **Community Life Gallery**: High-resolution gallery capturing worship, baptisms, fellowship, youth activities, and outreach.
- **Community Stories**: Testimonies from members about finding fellowship, experiencing spiritual growth, and partnering with our worldwide missions.
- **Connect & Prayer Form**: Online inquiry and prayer request form with Discord Tag / Roblox Username options and direct Discord & Roblox launch buttons (no WhatsApp or phone numbers required).
- **The Weekly Vision**: Newsletter sign-up for weekly devotionals and community announcements.
- **Footer**: Roblox & Discord venue details, Sunday prayer service schedule, pastoral contact, and missions giving information.

